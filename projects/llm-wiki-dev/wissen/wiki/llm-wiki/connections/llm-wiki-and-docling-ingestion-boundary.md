# LLM Wiki and Docling Ingestion Boundary

Cross-topic relationship:
- Docling centers on parsing and structured conversion from heterogeneous sources.
- LLM wiki workflows center on persistent synthesis, indexing, and maintenance after sources are normalized.

This creates a practical boundary: document-processing tools feed reliable markdown/text into `raw/`, while wiki operations transform those sources into durable conceptual structure.

See also: `wiki/docling/connections/docling-and-llm-wiki-workflows.md`.
