      <h2>Access to microdata</h2>
      <span></span>
    </div>
      
            <div property="schema:text">
        <p><strong>The Research Data Centres provide different possibilities for the access to official microdata. They differ on-site- and off-site ways of access.</strong></p>        <p>The ways of access presented below differ in terms of the <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="/en/anonymity">anonymity </a>of the usable data and in the way the data is provided. Data access is tied to certain conditions. You find further information in the <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="/en/terms-use">terms of use</a>. To use microdata, you have to know how to use one of the statistical analysis programs offered by the RDC (SAS, SPSS, Stata or R). To use the on-site ways of access or remote access, you have to work syntax-based.</p><p><strong>Note:</strong> Information on ways of access can also be found in the <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="https://www.forschungsdatenzentrum.de/de/lernkarten">RDC e-learning</a> (only available in German): Learning card 1 "FDZ allgemein" provides an overview, while learning card 3 "Zugangswege" offers a detailed description of the ways of access and a decision-making aid for choosing the appropriate way of access.</p><div class="row"><div class="col-md-4"><h3>On-site use:</h3><div class="accordion accordion-bg clearfix"><div class="acctitle acctitlec">Safe centre</div><div class="acc_content clearfix" style="display:block;"><p>There are PC workplaces for guest researchers in all locations of both Research Data Centres (safe centre). These can be used by researchers to analyse microdata inside the safe premises of the statistical offices. As the data is already protected through the regulation of data access and the equipment of the pc workplace, the microdata at the safe centre can be provided factually or formally <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="/en/anonymity">anonymised </a>– depending on the sensitivity of the data. Being obliged to statistical confidentiality in accordance with Section 16 Subcestion 7 of the Federal Statistics Act (BStatG) is a prerequisite for the usage of the safe centre.</p><p>The PC workplaces at which microdata can be analysed are equipped with the common statistical programs (SPSS, SAS, Stata and partly R). A separate PC workplace with internet connection is available for e-mail communication and internet searches. Several visits of a safe centre can take place within the scope of one project. Appointments have to be made in advance with the supervising RDC.</p><p>For the use of a safe centre it is required that all users are able to write program code in at least one analysis program offered at the RDC. The structure of the program code is defined in the <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="https://www.forschungsdatenzentrum.de/sites/default/files/rdc_sample_program_code.zip">sample program code</a>.</p></div><div class="acctitle">Remote execution</div><div class="acc_content clearfix" style="display:none;"><p>When using remote execution, the data user has no direct access to the data. The users rather receive data structure files which resemble the original material with regard to structure and variable values. With the help of these files, program codes can be prepared using the statistical programs SPSS, SAS, Stata or partly R. These program codes are applied by staff of the statistical offices to analyse the original data. The data users receive the results of those analyses after the relevant confidentiality checks.</p><p>Via remote execution, formally <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="/en/anonymity">anonymised microdata </a>can be analysed. Several program codes can be submitted within the scope of one project.</p><p>For the use of remote execution it is required that all users are able to write program code in at least one analysis program offered at the RDC. The structure of the program code is defined in the <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="https://www.forschungsdatenzentrum.de/sites/default/files/rdc_sample_program_code.zip">sample program code</a>.</p></div></div></div><div class="col-md-4"><h3>Remote Access:</h3><div class="accordion accordion-bg clearfix"><div class="acctitle acctitlec">Remote Scientific Use Files</div><div class="acc_content clearfix" style="display:block;"><p>Remote Scientific Use Files (Remote-SUF) are factually <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="/en/anonymity">anonymised</a> datasets from official statistics that can be accessed via the RDC's remote access system. Data use requires the requesting institution to be located in Germany and to implement specific technical and organizational requirements. Access to the remote access system is also permitted only on the premises of the requesting scientific institution within Germany. All data users must be obliged to statistical confidentiality in accordance with Section 16 Subcestion 7 of the Federal Statistics Act (BStatG).</p><p>Analyses via the remote access system are possible using either Stata or R. The chosen analysis software may impose limitations on the number of data users.</p><p>Further information about the Remote SUF, including an FAQ, can be found <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="https://www.forschungsdatenzentrum.de/en/remote-scientific-use-files">here</a>.</p></div></div></div><div class="col-md-4"><h3>Off-site use:</h3><div class="accordion accordion-bg clearfix"><div class="acctitle acctitlec">Scientific Use Files</div><div class="acc_content clearfix" style="display:block;"><p>Scientific Use Files (SUF) are standardised datasets which are generated by the Research Data Centres for popular statistics. Because of the factual <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="/en/anonymity">anonymization </a>of the microdata, they may be used outside the safe premises of the statistical offices. Compared to the on-site ways of access, SUF offer a lower potential for analyses. However, they are designed in a way that makes them suitable for a large part of scientific projects. Additionally, all dater users have to be obliged to statistical confidentiality in accordance with with Section 16 Subcestion 7 of the Federal Statistics Act (BStatG).</p><p>SUF are offered for the analysis programs SAS, SPSS and Stata or are provided with the according input routines. They may only be used within the scientific institution that requested the data.</p><p>Due to legal restrictions, SUF may only be used by researchers who are employed by a research institution that is registered and located in Germany. The use of Scientific Use Files may only take place in Germany. Researchers from foreign institutions are asked to use remote execution or to work at a safe centre.</p></div><div class="acctitle">Public Use Files</div><div class="acc_content clearfix" style="display:none;"><p>Public Use Files (PUF) are absolutely <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="/en/anonymity">anonymised microdata </a>. Because of the strong anonymization, only selected variables are available. Variables with a high degree of subject-related detail are aggregated. Detailed regional breakdowns can usually not be made based on Public Use Files. PUF can be downloaded for free after a successful registration. You can find an overview of all available PUF on the <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="/en/public-use-files">summary page</a>.</p><p>PUF are available for the analysis programs SAS, SPSS and Stata or are provided with the according input routines. Their use is not bound to a specific place of use.</p></div><div class="acctitle">Campus Files</div><div class="acc_content clearfix" style="display:none;"><p>The Research Data Centres of the Statistical Offices of the Federation and the Federal States encourage the use of official microdata in school and university teachings. Thus, they offer Campus Files which are designed specifically for teaching. They contain absolutely <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="/en/anonymity">anonymised microdata </a>– which pupils, students and otherwise interested persons can use to acquire methodological knowledge and gain first experiences in analysing official microdata.</p><p>Campus Files are offered for the analysis programs SAS, SPSS and Stata. They can be downloaded for free after successful registration. An overview on the <a class="internal icon-arrow-right" href="/en/campus-files">available Campus Files </a>– can be found on the summary page.</p></div></div></div></div><h3>Combined ways of access</h3><p>The described ways of access can also be combined freely. It is, for example, often useful to combine work at a safe centre and remote execution. Especially for longer research projects, a combination like this is often reasonable. Using combined ways of access usually results in extra costs.</p><h3>Non-scientific use of data</h3><p>According to section 16 (6) of the Federal Statistics Law (BStatG), the Research Data Centres may only grant “institutions of higher education or other institutions entrusted with tasks of independent scientific research” access to official microdata. Persons or institutions that are not covered by this definition can contact the enquiry services of the respective Statistical Offices of the Federation and the Federal States.
