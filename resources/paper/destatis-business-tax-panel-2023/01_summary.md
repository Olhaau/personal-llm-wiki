## Overview

The **Business-Tax-Panel** is a longitudinal microdata linkage project by the German Federal Statistical Office (DESTATIS) that combines corporate tax statistics (Ertragsteuerstatistiken) with VAT statistics (Umsatzsteuerstatistiken) for the same business entities over time.

## Problem Statement

Prior to this panel, empirical tax research with official German corporate tax data was limited:
- Tax statistics were available primarily as annual cross-sectional datasets
- The Research Data Centers (FDZ) offered merged cross-sections for specific years only
- A 2020 advisory report to the Federal Ministry of Finance identified significant data gaps for evidence-based tax policy

## Solution: The Business-Tax-Panel

The panel addresses these limitations by:
1. **Linking survey units over time** (longitudinal/panel structure)
2. **Combining multiple tax statistics** (cross-statistic analyses)
3. **Enabling comprehensive research** on corporate taxation

## Panel Specifications

| Characteristic | Value |
|----------------|-------|
| **Time Period** | 2013-2018 |
| **Total Observations** | >50 million |
| **Annual Average** | ~9 million observations |
| **Panel Type** | Unbalanced |
| **Variables Available** | >2,000 |

## Data Sources Integrated

The panel combines five tax statistics plus supplementary data:
1. **Gewerbesteuerstatistik** (Trade Tax Statistics)
2. **Körperschaftsteuerstatistik** (Corporate Income Tax Statistics)
3. **Statistik über Personengesellschaften und Gemeinschaften** (Partnership Statistics)
4. **Umsatzsteuerstatistik-Voranmeldungen** (VAT Advance Returns)
5. **Umsatzsteuerstatistik-Veranlagungen** (VAT Assessments)
6. **Einnahmenüberschussrechnung** (Income-Expense Statement - Supplementary)
7. **Statistisches Unternehmensregister** (Statistical Business Register - Supplementary)

## Key Capabilities

### Single-Statistic Analyses
- Longitudinal analysis of individual tax types
- Effects of tax law changes over time
- Tax burden calculations for specific entity types

### Cross-Statistic Analyses
- Relationship between corporate income and VAT
- Comprehensive corporate tax burden calculations
- Multi-dimensional business analysis

## Legal Framework

The linkage is explicitly authorized by the Tax Statistics Act (Steuerstatistikgesetz - StStatG):
- **§ 7a Abs. 1 StStatG**: Allows linking individual datasets across tax statistics for scientific analysis
- **§ 7a Abs. 2 StStatG**: Permits longitudinal studies of linked datasets

## Context: NeSt Network

The Business-Tax-Panel was developed in the context of the **Netzwerk empirische Steuerforschung (NeSt)** - the Network for Empirical Tax Research:
- Founded July 2023 at the inaugural conference
- Connects academia, tax administration, and official statistics
- Supported by Federal Minister of Finance and State Secretary

## Key Points

- First comprehensive longitudinal panel of German corporate tax statistics
- Enables both cross-sectional and longitudinal research designs
- Approximately half of observations contain data from 3+ statistics
- One-third contains data from 4+ statistics
- Will be available through the Research Data Centers (FDZ)

## Research Value

The panel enables research questions previously impossible with German administrative tax data:
- Tax policy impact evaluation
- Business dynamics and taxation
- Corporate tax burden analysis
- Cross-tax-type relationships
- Firm-level longitudinal studies
