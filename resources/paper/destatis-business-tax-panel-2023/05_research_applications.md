## Research Capabilities Overview

The Business-Tax-Panel enables multiple research designs:
- **Single-statistic longitudinal analyses**
- **Cross-statistic analyses**
- **Cross-sectional analyses**
- **Panel/longitudinal analyses**

## Income Tax (Ertragsteuern) Research Examples

### Trade Tax (Gewerbesteuer)
- Analysis of additions (Hinzurechnungen) and their impact on tax burden
- Municipal tax rate effects
- Trade income development over time

### Corporate Tax (Körperschaftsteuer)
- Effects of loss carryforward restrictions (Verlustabzugsbeschränkungen)
- Impact on corporate tax burden
- Income components and deductions

### Combined Income Tax Analysis
- **Comprehensive corporate tax burden calculation** for corporations
- Requires both trade tax and corporate tax data
- Possible for linked entities with complete information

## VAT Research

For VAT-specific research questions, the **Umsatzsteuerpanel** methodology (Harendt, 2021) provides detailed guidance:
- Crisis years analysis (2009-2012) demonstrates analytical potential
- VAT advance returns analysis
- Methods transferable to VAT assessments in Business-Tax-Panel
- Note: Shorter observation period limits some longitudinal analyses

## Cross-Tax Research

### Income Tax + VAT Combined
Coverage rates determine feasibility:

| Legal Form | Linkage Rate (2018) |
|------------|---------------------|
| Corporations | 67.9% |
| Partnerships | 50.5% |
| Sole proprietorships | 42.3% |
| Other legal entities | 26.6% |

**Key insight**: Linked entities represent 93-94% of total turnover despite lower count percentages.

### Fiscal Unity (Organkreis) Analysis

**Income Tax Fiscal Unity:**
- Corporate tax statistics capture both parents and subsidiaries
- Enables tracking annual changes in fiscal unity structures
- Complete visibility of income tax group arrangements

**VAT Fiscal Unity:**
- Use business register information (VAT statistics only include parent)
- Cross-reference with income tax fiscal unity for comparison
- Overlap and differences documented in Buchner et al. (2022) for 2016

**Important**: Full fiscal unity analysis requires complete business register linkage via AFiD-Panel Unternehmensregister.

## Expansion Through Business Statistics

### AFiD-Panel Unternehmensstrukturstatistiken (SBS-Panel)
Linkable via direct identifier (ID der wirtschaftlichen Einheit):

| Component | Content |
|-----------|---------|
| Kostenstrukturerhebung | Cost structure data |
| Investitionserhebungen | Investment surveys |
| Production statistics | Manufacturing sector |
| Environmental statistics | Resource usage |

### Expanded Research Possibilities
With business statistics linkage:
- Investment patterns and taxation
- Production factors and tax burden
- Value creation and tax incidence
- Cost structures and tax planning

### Methodological Considerations
- SBS-Panel partly sample-based with cutoff thresholds and rotation
- **Correct approach**: Use SBS-Panel as base, enrich with tax statistics (not reverse)
- Follow Kruse et al. (2021) methodology: Only supplement full enumeration data for sampled units

### Legal Restriction
**Partnership statistics and income-expense statement data CANNOT be linked to business statistics** (different legal basis - not classified as business statistics).

## Data Linkage Framework (Exkurs)

The paper presents a comprehensive framework showing three "blocks" of linkage possibilities:

### Block 1: Single-Statistic Longitudinal Only
Statistics that can only be analyzed over time individually, not linked with others:
- Lohn- und Einkommensteuer (for natural persons)
- Erbschaft- und Schenkungsteuer
- Grundsteuerwerte
- Lohnsteuerstatistik (Anmeldungen)

### Block 2: Cross-Statistic Linkage Permitted
Can be linked together and analyzed longitudinally as combined dataset:
- Umsatzsteuer
- Körperschaftsteuer (including E-Bilanz, EÜR)
- Gewerbesteuer (including E-Bilanz, EÜR)
- Country-by-Country Reporting statistics
- Forschungszulage (R&D tax credit)
- Erbschaft- und Schenkungsteuer
- Einkommensteuer (for businesses only)
- Grundsteuerwerte

**Plus:**
- Statistical register
- Data per Verwaltungsdatenverwendungsgesetz

### Block 3: Extended Linkage with Business/Environmental Statistics
Block 2 data can additionally be linked with:
- Business and environmental statistics (DESTATIS)
- Third-party publicly available data via common identifiers

### Future Data Sources (Planned)
*Shown in italics in the original - data DESTATIS will prepare in future:*
- Statistik zu länderbezogenen Berichten multinationaler Unternehmensgruppen
- Forschungszulage
- E-Bilanz (electronic balance sheet)

## Third-Party Data Linkage

### Potential External Sources
- Commercial balance sheet data (Handelsbilanz)
- Public company information
- Other administrative data with common identifiers

### Identifier Requirements
- Must have shared identifiers for Record Linkage
- Commercial register information
- Tax numbers (where accessible)
- Company identifiers

## Key Points

- Multiple research designs possible: cross-sectional, longitudinal, cross-statistic
- Linked entities represent vast majority of economic activity
- Legal framework determines which statistics can be combined
- Business statistics linkage expands possibilities but has legal limits
- Partnership statistics cannot be linked to business statistics
- Future data sources will further enhance analytical potential
- Third-party data linkage possible with common identifiers
