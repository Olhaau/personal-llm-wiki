## Summary of Achievements

The Business-Tax-Panel represents a significant advancement for German empirical tax research:

1. **First comprehensive longitudinal dataset** linking corporate tax and VAT statistics
2. **Cross-sectional and longitudinal** analysis capabilities
3. **Multiple research designs** enabled by single dataset
4. **Official administrative data** with complete enumeration quality

## Current Capabilities

### Available Analyses
- Single-statistic variable analysis over time
- Cross-statistic variable analysis
- Tax law change impact evaluation
- Corporate tax burden calculations
- Business dynamics and taxation studies

### Scope
- Time period: 2013-2018
- >50 million observations
- ~9 million annual observations
- >2,000 variables (with extension possible)

## Future Development Plans

### Regular Updates
- Panel will be **updated regularly**
- Methodological improvements ongoing

### Planned New Statistics Integration

| Statistic | Expected Benefit |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Forschungszulage** (R&D Tax Credit) | Innovation and taxation analysis |
| **Elektronische Bilanz** (E-Balance Sheet) | **Substantial information gain** - detailed balance sheet data |

### Historical Extension
- Earlier years could provide valuable insights
- Challenge: Income tax statistics only available triennially before 2011/2013
- Additional work required for backward extension

## Enhanced Research Through Linkage

### Manufacturing Sector Potential
For manufacturing companies, comprehensive panel datasets exist:
- Kostenstrukturerhebung (cost structure)
- Investitionserhebungen (investments)
- Produktionsstatistik (production)
- Umweltstatistiken (environmental)

**These can be enriched with Business-Tax-Panel data** for comprehensive business analysis.

### Third-Party Data
- Publicly available external data (e.g., commercial balance sheets)
- Linkable via common identifiers
- Expands research possibilities beyond official statistics

## Data Availability

### Access Channel
The Business-Tax-Panel will be made available through:
- **Forschungsdatenzentren der Statistischen Ämter des Bundes und der Länder (FDZ)**
- Scientific research access
- Data protection protocols apply

### Timeline
Available once all final preparations are complete (as of publication date: August 2023).

## Implications for Researchers

### Preparing for Access
1. Familiarize with FDZ application process
2. Understand legal framework for data linkage
3. Plan research design considering coverage rates
4. Consider which additional datasets may be needed

### Methodological Considerations
- Account for unbalanced panel structure
- Consider coverage differences by legal form
- Be aware of fiscal unity complexities
- Plan for potential missing data patterns

### Research Opportunities
The article explicitly aims to:
- Demonstrate the value of panel tax data
- Suggest interesting and in-depth analysis approaches
- Show the benefits of linking multiple data sources
- Encourage comprehensive research using the Business-Tax-Panel

## Key Points

- Regular updates and methodological improvements planned
- E-Balance Sheet integration promises substantial enhancements
- Manufacturing sector has rich complementary data available
- FDZ will be the access point for scientific research
- Backward extension possible but requires additional work
- Comprehensive research enabled by linkage capabilities
