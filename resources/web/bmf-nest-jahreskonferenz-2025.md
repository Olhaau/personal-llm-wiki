## Overview

The NeSt Annual Conference 2025 brought together representatives from academia, official statistics, administration, and politics to discuss:
- How to advance empirical tax research
- Better data linkage for evidence-based policy advice
- Insights from high-quality administrative data
- International comparison for scientific access to tax data

### Key Speakers
- **Staatssekretär Dr. Rolf Bösinger** (BMF) - Conference Chair
- **Parlamentarischer Staatssekretär Michael Schrodi** (BMF)
