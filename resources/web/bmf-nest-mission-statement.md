## Document Purpose

The Mission Statement defines the political mandate, goals, and approach of the Network for Empirical Tax Research (Netzwerk empirische Steuerforschung, NeSt). It was signed by Federal Finance Minister Christian Lindner and State Secretary Prof. Dr. Luise Hölscher at the NeSt launch conference on July 20, 2023.

---

## Core Rationale

### Role of Taxes
- Taxes are an essential source of state revenue for providing public goods
- Taxes finance public services and government operations
- Taxes can influence economic decisions of individuals and companies

### Need for Evidence-Based Policy
For evidence-based policy advice and legislation, it is essential to analyze the effects of taxes on:
1. **Public finances** (Staatshaushalt)
2. **Economic decisions** of individuals and businesses
3. **Distribution of income and wealth** in society

### Data Requirements
Scientifically sound analysis of tax effects is only empirically possible when:
- Legal frameworks are respected
- Suitable primary data from the entire taxation process is properly prepared
- Tax data can be appropriately utilized

## Network Definition

> *"Das Netzwerk empirische Steuerforschung (NeSt) ist eine unter dem Dach des Bundesministeriums der Finanzen errichtete und von ihm betriebene offene und interdisziplinär ausgerichtete Plattform."*

**Translation:** NeSt is an open, interdisciplinary platform established and operated under the auspices of the Federal Ministry of Finance.

### Primary Function
Connecting empirical researchers in the field of taxation with:
- Official statistics (amtliche Statistik)
- Tax administration (Finanzverwaltung)

The network brings these groups together with their different areas of expertise.

---

## Central Goals

### 1. Improve Data Infrastructure
- Identify and analyze problems, opportunities, and usage potential of tax data
- Examine tax data structures
- Develop and implement strategies for targeted advancement of data infrastructure
- Execute concrete projects

### 2. Enhance Knowledge Exchange
- Intensify knowledge and experience exchange among experts
- Create dialogue formats
- Foster interdisciplinary collaboration

### 3. Support Evidence-Based Legislation
- Improve the data basis for scientifically founded answers to questions
- Enable better evaluation of tax regulations
- Support legally mandated evaluation of legislative proposals

### 4. Promote Research Projects
Support specific projects for empirical analysis of tax questions through:
- Networking
- Financial support
- Scientific methods application

## Research Areas

Specific research questions may emerge from dialogue among participants and institutions, covering:

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Evaluation of tax regulations** | Assessing effectiveness of existing tax rules |
| **Burden effects** (Belastungswirkungen) | Impact on taxpayers |
| **Tax revenues** | Revenue estimation and analysis |
| **Taxation processes** | Administrative procedures and efficiency |
| **Behavioral effects** | How taxes influence economic behavior |

## Policy Integration

### Federal Data Strategy
NeSt aligns with the German Federal Government's data strategy (Datenstrategie der Bundesregierung).

### Legislative Evaluation
NeSt improves conditions for the legally required evaluation of legislative proposals.

### Administrative Modernization
Research outcomes should contribute to:
- Better data use within administration
- Advancing digitalization
- Reducing bureaucracy (Entbürokratisierung)

---

## Membership

### Open Participation
Participation in NeSt activities is open to all professionally interested individuals.

### Personal Membership
Experts working in empirical tax research or related fields who:
- Support the network's goals
- Wish to engage in ongoing exchange within NeSt

may join as personal members.

### Research Standards
NeSt activities adhere to:
- DFG (German Research Foundation) research-oriented justice and diversity standards
- DFG guidelines for safeguarding good scientific practice
- Public dissemination of research results (where not restricted by other requirements)

---

## Societal Contribution

The knowledge gained through NeSt should:
1. **Serve societal progress**
2. **Address societal challenges**
3. **Strengthen Germany as a research location** (Wissenschaftsstandort Deutschland)
4. **Advance empirical tax research** in Germany

## Implementation Notes for Agents

### Key Takeaways
1. NeSt is the official German government initiative for improving tax research data infrastructure
2. It operates under BMF auspices but connects academia, statistics, and administration
3. The mission emphasizes evidence-based policy making through better data
4. Research projects can receive both networking and financial support

### Relevance for Data Analysis
- NeSt is the primary channel for accessing improved tax microdata in Germany
- Projects supported by NeSt may produce new datasets (e.g., Business Tax Panel)
- Collaboration with Destatis FDZ is facilitated through NeSt
