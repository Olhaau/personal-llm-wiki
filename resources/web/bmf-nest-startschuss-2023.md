## Overview

The Network for Empirical Tax Research (Netzwerk empirische Steuerforschung, NeSt) is an interdisciplinary open platform established under the auspices of the German Federal Ministry of Finance (BMF). It connects three key disciplines:

1. **Academia** (Wissenschaft)
2. **Official Statistics** (Statistik)
3. **Tax Administration** (Verwaltung)

The network aims to improve data infrastructure and contribute to evidence-based policy advice in the field of empirical tax research.

## Mission and Goals

### Core Objectives

1. **Improve data infrastructure** for tax research
2. **Enable evidence-based policy advice** through better data utilization
3. **Facilitate knowledge transfer** between disciplines
4. **Support administrative modernization** through data-driven approaches
5. **Strengthen Germany as a research location** (Wissenschaftsstandort)

### Key Activities

- Transform tax data into knowledge and insights through systematic analysis
- Create structures for cooperation between stakeholders
- Organize knowledge and communication formats
- Support both material tax law development and tax administration/enforcement

---

## Organizational Structure

### BMF Role
The BMF acts as the network coordinator:
- Links network members
- Creates structures for cooperation
- Organizes knowledge and communication formats
- Provides central coordination function

### Governance Bodies

| Body | Function |
|------|----------|
| **Geschäftsstelle (Office)** | Coordinates network activities, connects stakeholders, supports project implementation |
| **Lenkungsausschuss (Steering Committee)** | Advises on research priorities, recommends projects, evaluates funded projects, supports external representation |

### Member Composition
- Universities and research institutes
- Federal Statistical Office (Statistisches Bundesamt) and its Research Data Centre (FDZ)
- Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt)
- General Customs Directorate (Generalzolldirektion)
- State tax administrations (Landesfinanzverwaltungen)

## Launch Conference Highlights

### Day 1: Network Foundation

**Opening by State Secretary Prof. Dr. Luise Hölscher**
- Key message: "We must know mutually what we know!"
- Approach: View the network as an "agile experiment"

**Key Presentations:**

1. **Scientific Advisory Board Perspective** (Prof. Dr. Thiess Büttner, Prof. Dr. Andreas Peichl)
   - Use methodological advances to analyze tax system effects
   - Increase credibility of research results
   - Improve data infrastructure

2. **BMF Perspective** (Dr. Nils Weith)
   - Network offers flexible, bureaucracy-reduced approach
   - Better than creating another formal institution
   - Focus on sustainable data infrastructure and interdisciplinary knowledge transfer

3. **Lessons from Other Networks** (Prof. Stefan Bender, Deutsche Bundesbank; Brigitte Loose, FNA)
   - Balance data usage costs with research impact
   - Research provides "self-defense" and legitimation for policy decisions

## Business Tax Panel (BTP) - First NeSt Product

The Business Tax Panel is the first product initiated through NeSt, presented by Annette Kristiansen (Destatis).

### Data Sources
- Gewerbesteuerstatistik (trade tax)
- Körperschaftsteuerstatistik (corporate tax)
- Umsatzsteuerstatistik (VAT)
- Statistik über Personengesellschaften und Gemeinschaften (partnerships)
- Einnahmen-Überschuss-Rechnung (income-expenditure accounting)
- Unternehmensregister (business register)

### Characteristics
- **Time coverage:** 2013-2018 (at launch)
- **Structure:** Unbalanced panel (entities not required in every year/statistic)
- **Analysis capability:** Both cross-sectional and longitudinal analysis

## Implementation Notes for Agents

### Context
NeSt is the central German initiative for improving tax research data infrastructure. It is particularly relevant for:
- Understanding German tax statistics data access
- Research collaboration opportunities with Destatis FDZ
- Evidence-based tax policy development in Germany

### Key Connections
- **FDZ Destatis:** Research Data Centre providing microdata access
- **Business Tax Panel (BTP):** First concrete data product
- **E-Bilanz data:** Expected availability from 2026 (reporting year 2021)
- **KONSENS/DAME:** Federal-state data warehouse for tax data

### Timeline
- July 2023: Launch conference
- 2024: First annual conference
- 2026: E-Bilanz data expected to be available
