## Context

The **Netzwerk empirische Steuerforschung (NeSt)** is a platform hosted by the German Federal Ministry of Finance for networking between academia, official statistics, and tax administration. Since February 2025, virtual exchange formats (webinars) cover various content and methodological topics in empirical tax research.
