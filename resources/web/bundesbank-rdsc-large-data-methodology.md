## Context

This technical report is published by the **Research Data and Service Centre (RDSC)** of the Deutsche Bundesbank. The RDSC provides access to microdata from the Bundesbank's data collections for academic research purposes.

### About the RDSC

The Research Data and Service Centre is part of the Bundesbank's Research and Research Data division. It offers:

- Access to research data for external researchers
- Research project support
- Methodology reports and documentation
- Part of the INEXDA network (International Network for Exchanging Experiences on Statistical Handling of Granular Data)
