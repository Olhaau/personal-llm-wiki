# BSI Grundschutz Softwareentwicklung

## Summary

BSI Grundschutz software development combines IT-Grundschutz methodik, governance standards, and software-focused bausteine for secure planning, implementation, and operation [[raw/bsi-it-grundschutz-kompendium.md]]. The 200-series standards define management and risk framing, while software bausteine specify implementation controls [[raw/bsi-standard-200-2-it-grundschutz-methodik.md]]. Together they establish a governance-to-engineering chain for security-relevant software work [[raw/bsi-con-8-software-entwicklung.md]].

## Details

Operationalization depends on mapping high-level ISMS requirements from standards 200-1 to 200-4 to concrete software controls such as secure development, hardening, and lifecycle checks [[raw/bsi-standard-200-1-isms.md]]. APP.6 and CON.8 provide software-near control guidance that can be integrated into release planning, testing, and change management [[raw/bsi-app-6-allgemeine-software.md]].
