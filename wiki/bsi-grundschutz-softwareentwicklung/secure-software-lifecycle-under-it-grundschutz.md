# Secure Software Lifecycle under IT-Grundschutz

An IT-Grundschutz-oriented software lifecycle view combines:
- governance and methodik (ISMS and risk process from BSI 200-series),
- development controls (CON.8), and
- operational software controls over the lifecycle (APP.6).

This creates a bridge from policy and management intent to concrete software engineering and operational practice.
