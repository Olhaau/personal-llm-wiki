# Docling and LLM Wiki Workflows

Docling and the LLM wiki pattern connect at the ingestion boundary:
- Docling focuses on robust parsing/conversion from heterogeneous source formats.
- LLM wiki workflows focus on persistent synthesis, indexing, and cross-reference maintenance after source normalization.

In practice, Docling-like tools can serve as upstream normalization for `raw/`, while wiki compile/query/lint loops operate on markdown outputs and references.
