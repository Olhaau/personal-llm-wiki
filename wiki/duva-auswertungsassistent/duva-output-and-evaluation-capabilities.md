# DUVA Output and Evaluation Capabilities

The DUVA Auswertungsassistent is described as a user-oriented evaluation layer that transforms datasets into readable outputs without requiring specialized EDV expertise.

Across DUVA product pages, output capabilities are framed as tables, lists, charts, and maps, with publication in an information portal for internal and external stakeholders.
