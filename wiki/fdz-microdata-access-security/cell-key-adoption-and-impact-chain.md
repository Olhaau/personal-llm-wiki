# Cell-Key Adoption and Impact Chain

The newly ingested "statistische geheimhaltung" sources describe a method chain:

- criteria-based evaluation of secrecy procedures,
- adoption of Cell-Key as a scalable confidentiality approach,
- operationalization in FDZ and official publication contexts,
- documented effects on result interpretation (for example non-additivity, small random deviations).

This connects governance-level method decisions to downstream user-facing data quality behavior.
