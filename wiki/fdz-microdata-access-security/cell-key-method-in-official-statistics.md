# Cell-Key Method in Official Statistics

The Cell-Key method is presented as a data-perturbation confidentiality mechanism that introduces controlled uncertainty into published outputs while preserving analytical usability.

Across Destatis and Statistikportal sources, the method is framed as a practical balance between:
- legal secrecy obligations,
- flexible tabulation and database use,
- bounded information loss,
- and predictable quality impacts (for example non-additivity and small deviations).
