# FDZ Access to microdata

The page explains access channels (safe centre, remote execution, Remote SUF, SUF, PUF, Campus Files) and associated controls.

Security-relevant points:
- access path depends on anonymity level and legal constraints,
- on-site and remote execution include output confidentiality checks,
- use of Remote SUF and SUF is restricted to eligible institutions in Germany,
- users must be committed to statistical confidentiality,
- syntax-based analysis environments and controlled workflows reduce uncontrolled data exposure.
