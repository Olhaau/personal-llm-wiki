# FDZ Terms of use

The page defines legal and contractual conditions for access to official microdata via the Research Data Centres.

Key points:
- access is legally regulated (BStatG), project-bound, temporary, and contract-based,
- users and institutions must be eligible and bound to statistical confidentiality,
- confidentiality protection and output checks are mandatory,
- misuse and re-identification attempts can lead to prosecution and exclusion,
- user contracts define rights/obligations and technical/organizational use constraints.
