# Wiki Section Index

Top-level index for wiki sections.

## Sections

- [public](./public/_index.md) - publishable wiki content that can be shared.
- [intern](./intern/_index.md) - private internal wiki content.

## Boundary Rules

- `wiki/public/` must not reference or link to `wiki/intern/`.
- `wiki/intern/` may reference `wiki/public/` where needed.
