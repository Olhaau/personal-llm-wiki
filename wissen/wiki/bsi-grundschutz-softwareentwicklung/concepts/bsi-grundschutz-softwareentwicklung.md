# BSI Grundschutz Softwareentwicklung

`bsi-grundschutz-softwareentwicklung` is the topic-level concept combining BSI IT-Grundschutz methodik, governance standards, and software-centric bausteine for secure software planning, implementation, and operation.

In this wiki, it acts as the anchor between high-level ISMS governance (BSI 200-series) and concrete software requirements from IT-Grundschutz bausteine.
