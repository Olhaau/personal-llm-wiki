# Docling

Docling is the core concept for the `docling` topic: a document-processing stack for converting heterogeneous inputs into structured, downstream-ready representations used by AI workflows.

Within this wiki, Docling is treated as the source-normalization layer that enables reliable ingestion into persistent knowledge systems.
