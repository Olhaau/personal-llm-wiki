# llm-wiki

`llm-wiki` is the core concept for this topic: a persistent, compounding knowledge-base pattern where an LLM incrementally compiles and maintains interlinked wiki pages between users and raw sources.

The central shift is from query-time rediscovery to ongoing synthesis, maintenance, and traceable updates.
