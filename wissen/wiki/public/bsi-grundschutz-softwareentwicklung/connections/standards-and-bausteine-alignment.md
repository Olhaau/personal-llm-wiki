# Standards and Bausteine Alignment

The BSI software-security stack aligns in two layers:
- Governance layer: BSI 200-1 to 200-4 define ISMS, methodik, risk, and continuity structure.
- Execution layer: IT-Grundschutz bausteine (especially CON.8 and APP.6) define concrete software-development and software-lifecycle requirements.

This alignment enables organizations to connect strategic security management with implementable controls in software projects and operations.
