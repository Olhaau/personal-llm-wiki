# Legal and Contractual Access Controls

RDC microdata access is controlled by legal provisions (notably BStatG references) and a contract model that binds users, institutions, purpose, duration, and operational conditions.

The control model includes:
- eligibility checks for institutions and users,
- project-bound requests with defined methods and data scope,
- formal agreement to confidentiality obligations,
- temporary and purpose-limited use with extensions under defined conditions.
